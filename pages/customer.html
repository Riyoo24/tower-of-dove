            Renting equipment to take outside the studio is for registered customers only. Log in or create an account to continue. Guests can still add equipment to a studio booking.

            * Required

            How outside rental time works
            • The price covers up to 8 hours of use from your pick-up time. Use it the full 8 hours (for example 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM) at no extra charge.
            • You may return earlier than 8 hours (for example 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM). The price stays the same, it does not go down.
            • Every hour beyond 8 hours adds one-eighth of the item's price.
            • A late fee only starts after your full 8-hour window has passed, even if you scheduled an earlier return time.
